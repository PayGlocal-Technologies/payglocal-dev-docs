<Steps>
  <Step title="Customer clicks Pay">
    The customer clicks a payment link, invoice link, or Pay button on the merchant site.
  </Step>
  <Step title="PayGlocal hosted checkout">
    PayGlocal displays a branded checkout page with the payment methods configured for the merchant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customer selects payment method">
    Card, UPI, netbanking, or wallet — available methods depend on merchant configuration and customer device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="3DS / OTP authentication">
    For card payments, the customer completes 3D Secure or OTP verification with their bank.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirmation">
    The customer sees a success or failure screen. The merchant receives an x-gl-token callback to their `merchantCallbackURL`.
  </Step>
</Steps>
