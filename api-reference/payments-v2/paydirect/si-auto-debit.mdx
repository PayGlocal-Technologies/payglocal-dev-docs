## AUTO DEBIT standing instructions

**AUTO DEBIT** standing instructions automatically process subsequent debits based on the configured schedule after successful mandate registration.

Registration uses the same PayDirect initiate endpoint as [GPI](/api-reference/payments-v2/paydirect/gpi) with a `standingInstruction` block.

### Key points

- Register the mandate using the **Initiate API** with **`startDate`** and a scheduled **`frequency`** such as `WEEKLY`, `BIWEEKLY`, `MONTHLY`, `QUARTERLY`, `HALFYEARLY`, or `YEARLY` (not `ONDEMAND`).
- After successful registration, subsequent debit transactions are **automatically initiated** according to the configured schedule.
- **No [SI sale API](/api-reference/standing-instructions/si-subsequent-payment) calls** are required for subsequent debit transactions.
- The amount for subsequent debits must be **`FIXED`**.

<Warning>
  When **Auto Debit** is enabled for your MID, `ONDEMAND` frequency and `VARIABLE` type are not supported.
</Warning>

---

## When to Use

Use this endpoint when you need to register a recurring debit mandate for **international cards** where the charge amount and schedule are known upfront and do not vary. Auto-debit is appropriate for subscription billing, EMI plans, or any fixed-amount recurring charge where you want PayGlocal to initiate each debit automatically on your behalf.

Choose [on-demand SI](/api-reference/standing-instructions/si-subsequent-payment) instead if the amount or timing of each charge may differ, or if your MID does not have Auto Debit enabled. Do not use this flow for domestic cards or token-based payments — `cardData` with a raw international PAN is required.

## Supported payment methods

| Method | Domestic | International |
|--------|:--------:|:-------------:|
| Cards | — | ✓ |

Standing instructions support **international cards** only. In **Request body** → `paymentData`, send **`cardData` only** (`tokenData` is not supported).

## Headers

<Snippet file="paydirect-request-headers.mdx" />

Set `standingInstruction.data` (`amount`, scheduled `frequency`, `type: FIXED`, `startDate`) in **Request body** below.

<Note>

</Note>

## Error Scenarios

| Scenario | HTTP Code |
|----------|-----------|
| Invalid or missing request fields (e.g. missing `startDate`, wrong `type`) | 400 |
| Card domicile or card type not configured for your MID (`GL-201-020`) | 400 |
| Duplicate `merchantUniqueId` — same ID sent more than once (`GL-400-001`) | 429 |
| Merchant is blacklisted and not permitted to transact (`GL-403-001`) | 403 |
| Authentication failure — invalid or expired JWS token | 403 |
