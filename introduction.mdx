## What You Can Build

PayGlocal is an RBI-licensed payment aggregator. These docs cover three products:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Payment Gateway" icon="credit-card" href="/payments/overview">
    Accept one-time and recurring payments. PayCollect (hosted) or PayDirect (seamless).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Merchant Onboarding" icon="user-check" href="/onboarding/overview">
    Programmatically onboard sub-merchants onto the PayGlocal platform.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Multi-Currency Accounts" icon="globe" href="/mca/concepts">
    Receive foreign currency collections and settle in INR.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
