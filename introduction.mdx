## Welcome to PayGlocal

PayGlocal is an RBI-regulated payment gateway based in India, offering payment collection services to merchants and partners across global and domestic markets.

This portal serves two distinct audiences. Pick the path that matches your role:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="I'm a Partner — onboarding sub-merchants" icon="building-columns" href="/api-reference/onboarding-overview">
    You operate a platform and onboard sub-merchants onto PayGlocal programmatically. Use the **Partner: Merchant Onboarding API** to submit KYC data, upload documents, configure products, and trigger VKYC verification.
  </Card>
  <Card title="I'm a Merchant — accepting payments" icon="credit-card" href="/guides/integration-chooser">
    You accept payments from your customers. Use the **Merchant Payment APIs**, **Payment Links** (no-code), or an **E-commerce Plugin** (Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, OpenCart, Wix).
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  Shared sections — **Getting Started**, **Security Setup**, and **Reference** — apply to both audiences. Persona-specific sections in the sidebar are prefixed **Partner:** or **Merchant:** so you can focus on what's relevant to you.
</Note>

## How Integration Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up keys and authentication">
    Generate your Private Key and download PayGlocal's Public Certificate from the Control Center. Configure your client to sign and encrypt every request.

    → [Key Management Overview](/key-management/overview)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your integration path">
    Decide between PayCollect (hosted, no PCI required), PayDirect (custom checkout, PCI required), Standalone Auth/Capture, or Standing Instructions for recurring payments.

    → [Which Integration Is Right for You?](/guides/integration-chooser)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Onboard your first merchant">
    Make your first API call to create an onboarding record, then submit business details, documents, and trigger verification.

    → [Merchant Onboarding Flow](/guides/api-flow-overview)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Integrate payments">
    Initiate your first GPI payment, handle the redirect callback, and check transaction status.

    → [Payment Flow Overview](/integration/payment-flow)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live">
    Complete sandbox testing, get written go-live approval from PayGlocal, and switch to the Production environment.

    → [Requirements & Compliance](/requirements)
  </Step>
</Steps>
