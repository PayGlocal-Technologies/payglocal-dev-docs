## Prerequisites

- Merchant APPROVED in PayGlocal onboarding
- MCA product activated (contact your account manager with desired currencies)
- x-api-key provisioned (provided by account manager)

<Note>MCA APIs use x-api-key header authentication, not the RSA JWS signing used by payment APIs.</Note>

## Step 1: Fetch Virtual Accounts

Call GET /mca/accounts to retrieve your virtual account details.

```bash
GET /mca/accounts
x-api-key: <API_KEY>
```

The response contains one virtual account per enabled currency + collection label combination. Each account includes:
- Virtual account number
- Bank name and branch
- SWIFT/routing codes
- Currency
- Collection label

**Cache these accounts.** Refresh daily or when you receive a 404 (account reassigned).

## Step 2: Share with Payers

Provide your foreign payer with:
- Virtual account number
- Bank name and SWIFT code
- Any reference field your payer requires

Funds are credited to your virtual account when the payer's SWIFT/wire transfer is processed.

## Step 3: Receive MCA_FUND_RECEIVED Webhook

Configure your webhook endpoint to receive MCA events (see [MCA API Integration](/mca/api)). When funds arrive, PayGlocal POSTs an MCA_FUND_RECEIVED event to your webhook URL.

After receiving this event:
1. Acknowledge with HTTP 200
2. Call GET /mca/accounts to confirm the credited amount
3. Proceed with document upload for FIRC compliance
