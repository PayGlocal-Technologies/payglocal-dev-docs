## What Is a Multi-Currency Account?

A PayGlocal Multi-Currency Account (MCA) is a virtual bank account provisioned by PayGlocal that accepts inward foreign currency remittances. PayGlocal assigns a dedicated virtual account number and local routing details (ABA, BSB, Sort Code, IBAN, etc.) per currency per merchant. When a foreign payer sends funds to that account, PayGlocal converts at the prevailing FX rate and credits the INR equivalent to the merchant's registered bank account.

## How Collections Work

<Steps>
  <Step title="Virtual accounts assigned">
    After a merchant is fully onboarded and activated, PayGlocal provisions virtual bank accounts for each enabled currency. Retrieve them via [Fetch Account Details](/mca/account-fetch).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Merchant shares account details">
    The merchant shares the virtual account number and local routing codes with their overseas payer. The payer sends a local bank transfer — no SWIFT fees, no cross-border friction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Funds received">
    PayGlocal credits the virtual account and the transaction enters `DOCUMENT_PENDING` status. A webhook fires to notify your system.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invoice uploaded">
    Your integration uploads the supporting invoice using [Upload Documents](/mca/document-upload). PayGlocal uses this to satisfy RBI's purpose-of-remittance requirements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="FX conversion and INR settlement">
    PayGlocal converts the foreign currency at the prevailing rate and settles INR to the merchant's registered Indian bank account. Settlement typically completes within T+1 or T+2 business days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="FIRC generated">
    A Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) is generated. The transaction moves to `FIRC_RECEIVED` status and the certificate is delivered via webhook.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Transaction Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| `DOCUMENT_PENDING` | Funds received; waiting for the supporting invoice to be uploaded |
| `SENT_FOR_SETTLEMENT` | Invoice accepted; FX conversion and settlement are in progress |
| `SETTLED` | INR credited to the merchant's bank account |
| `FIRC_RECEIVED` | FIRC generated and available |

## Virtual Account Details

Each virtual account includes:

- **Account number** — the unique account identifier in the destination country
- **Routing code** — country-specific routing information (ABA for USD, BSB for AUD, Sort Code for GBP, IBAN/BIC for EUR, etc.)
- **Bank name and address** — required by some payers when initiating a wire
- **Currency** — the currency this account accepts

<Note>Virtual account details are read-only. They are assigned by PayGlocal during merchant activation and cannot be changed by the merchant or partner.</Note>

## Collection Labels

Each virtual account can carry a `collection_label` — a merchant-defined string (for example, `US_SALES` or `EU_SERVICES`) used to segregate fund flows by business unit or product line. Labels are set during MCA activation and are returned in account-fetch and webhook payloads.

## FX and Settlement

PayGlocal applies the prevailing interbank FX rate at the time of conversion. The exact rate and INR amount are included in the `SETTLED` webhook payload. All settlements are in INR, credited to the merchant's registered Indian bank account.

<Warning>FX rates are not locked in at the time funds are received. The rate applied is the rate prevailing when PayGlocal processes the conversion, which occurs after the supporting invoice is accepted.</Warning>

## Supported Currencies

MCA supports major currencies including USD, EUR, GBP, and AUD. The exact list of enabled currencies for your merchant is determined at onboarding. Contact your account manager to add currencies after activation.

## Eligibility

A merchant must be in `APPROVED` status in the PayGlocal onboarding system before MCA can be activated. Contact your account manager to enable MCA and specify the currencies required.

## FIRC (Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate)

A FIRC is a certificate issued to confirm receipt of foreign currency into India. RBI mandates that merchants retain FIRCs for foreign currency inflows. PayGlocal generates a FIRC after each MCA settlement and delivers it via the `FIRC_RECEIVED` webhook event.

<Note>FIRCs are generated by PayGlocal's banking partners and are non-editable. If a FIRC is missing or needs to be re-requested, contact PayGlocal support with the transaction reference.</Note>

## Authentication

MCA APIs use the Onboarding API auth scheme — HMAC-SHA256 with `x-gl-auth` and `x-gl-digest` request headers. See [Key Management](/set-up/keys/overview) for how to generate these headers.
