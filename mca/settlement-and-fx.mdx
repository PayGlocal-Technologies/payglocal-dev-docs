## Settlement Flow

1. Foreign payer sends funds to your PayGlocal virtual account
2. PayGlocal receives and credits the foreign currency amount
3. PayGlocal converts at the prevailing FX rate
4. INR equivalent is credited to your registered bank account (T+1 or T+2 business days)
5. FIRC is generated and delivered via webhook

## Settlement Currency

All MCA settlements are in **INR**, regardless of the collection currency. The FX rate applied and the resulting INR amount are included in the `TXN_SETTLED` webhook payload — use these fields for reconciliation.

<Note>Contact your account manager for exact settlement SLAs for your currency pair and volume tier.</Note>

## FX Rate

PayGlocal applies its prevailing interbank FX rate at the time each settlement batch is processed. The exact rate is returned in the `fxRate` field of the `TXN_SETTLED` webhook payload.

```json
{
  "gid": "glm123456789",
  "merchantId": "<MERCHANT_ID>",
  "transaction_amount": "1000.00",
  "transaction_currency": "USD",
  "settled_at": "2024-10-23",
  "fxRate": "91.39",
  "settlement_amount": "998.00",
  "settlement_currency": "INR",
  "status": "SETTLED"
}
```

`settlement_amount` is the INR credit after FX conversion. `fxRate` is the USD/INR rate applied at batch time.

## Webhook Events

| Event | Status field | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| `MCA_FUND_RECEIVED` | `DOCUMENT_PENDING` | Foreign currency funds credited to the virtual account |
| `TXN_SENT_FOR_SETTLEMENT` | `SENT_FOR_SETTLEMENT` | Invoice accepted; FX conversion and INR transfer queued |
| `TXN_SETTLED` | `SETTLED` | INR credited to merchant bank account; includes `fxRate` and `settlement_amount` |
| `FIRC_RECEIVED` | `FIRC_RECEIVED` | FIRC document generated; includes a 10-minute download URL |

See [Webhook Events](/mca/webhooks) for full payload schemas and handling guidance.

## FIRC

A Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) is generated after each `TXN_SETTLED` event. The `FIRC_RECEIVED` webhook contains a `fircUrl` that expires after **10 minutes**.

<Warning>
  Download and store the FIRC document to your own storage as soon as you receive `FIRC_RECEIVED`. The `fircUrl` is not valid after 10 minutes — do not cache the URL and fetch later.
</Warning>

Retain FIRCs for RBI compliance. They serve as official proof of receipt for cross-border inward remittances.
