Once a payment is **authorized**, funds are held on the customer's card but not yet collected. Use the capture endpoints when you are ready to fulfill, or reversal to release the hold without charging.

```
POST /gl/v1/payments/{gid}/capture   →  Full or partial capture
POST /gl/v1/payments/{gid}/reversal  →  Release hold without charging
```

| Action | When to use |
|---|---|
| [Auth Full Capture](/merchant/auth-capture-guide#auth-full-capture) | Collect the entire authorized amount |
| [Auth Partial Capture](/merchant/auth-capture-guide#auth-partial-capture) | Collect less than authorized; remainder is released |
| [Auth Reversal](/merchant/auth-capture-guide#auth-reversal) | Cancel before capture — no charge occurs |

<Warning>
  Auth Reversal applies only to **AUTHORIZED** transactions that have not been captured. If funds were already captured, use the standard refund flow instead.
</Warning>
