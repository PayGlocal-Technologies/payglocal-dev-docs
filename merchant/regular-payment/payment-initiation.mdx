Initiate a regular payment using one of two endpoints depending on your integration type.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Checkout Flow (PayCollect)" icon="window" color="#1A6FE8">
    ```
    POST /gl/v1/payments/initiate/paycollect
    ```
    You send amount and currency. PayGlocal redirects the customer to a hosted checkout page to enter card details and complete payment. The result is POSTed back to your `merchantCallbackURL`.

    **Supports:** Cards, Apple Pay, UPI, Net Banking and Alternative Payment Methods.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Seamless Flow (PayDirect)" icon="code" color="#8B5CF6">
    ```
    POST /gl/v1/payments/initiate
    ```
    Your frontend collects card details and your backend sends them directly to PayGlocal. No redirect — payment is processed entirely on your domain.

  </Card>
</CardGroup>

For full payload reference and side-by-side comparison, see [Checkout Flow vs Seamless Flow](/merchant/api-integration).
