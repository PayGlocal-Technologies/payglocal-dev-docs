## SI Activate

Resume a paused mandate. The mandate becomes active immediately and the next scheduled charge proceeds as normal.

---

## Business Use Cases

- Customer resolves payment issue
- Dispute settled in your favor
- Seasonal service resumes
- Customer updates payment method successfully
- Free trial or payment holiday ends

---

## Endpoint

```
PUT /gl/v1/payments/si/status
```

### Request

```json
{
  "merchantTxnId": "ACTIVATE-23AEE8CB6B62EE2AF07",
  "standingInstruction": {
    "action":    "ACTIVATE",
    "mandateId": "md_94f0bb40-2664-4851-ab83-b86c618d3e15"
  }
}
```

---

## What Happens After Activation

- Mandate becomes active immediately
- Next scheduled charge proceeds as normal
- All original mandate details preserved
- Billing cycle continues from where it left off

---

## When to Activate

<Warning>
  Only activate after confirming the reason for pause is resolved. Verify with the customer when appropriate. Ensure payment method is still valid and has sufficient funds.
</Warning>
