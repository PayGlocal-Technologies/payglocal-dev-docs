## Prerequisites

- A GCC (Glocal Command Center) account. If you do not have one, contact your PayGlocal account manager.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to GCC">
    Sign in to the [GCC dashboard](https://merchant.payglocal.in) with your credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Payment Link">
    In the left navigation pane, click **Payment Link**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new link">
    Click the **Create Payment Link** button (top right).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill the form">
    Enter the Payment Link details (see [fields](#payment-link-fields) below).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Click **Generate Link**. The dashboard displays the generated Payment Link ID (PLID) and the shareable URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share">
    PayGlocal automatically emails and/or SMSes the link to the customer contact you provided. You can also copy the URL and share it over any other channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm delivery">
    You will receive a confirmation email once the link is dispatched to the customer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Customers can attempt payment on a Payment Link up to **10 times**. After 10 failed attempts, the link status changes to **Exhausted** and can no longer be used.
</Warning>

## Payment Link fields

| Field | Required | Default | Notes |
|-------|----------|---------|-------|
| Currency | Yes | `INR` | Currency of the payment. |
| Amount | Yes | — | Amount to be collected. |
| Note | No | — | Description shown to the customer on the checkout page. |
| Expiry | No | 2 hours | Link validity. Maximum 48 hours. |
| Customer Email | Conditional | — | Required if Customer Mobile is not provided. |
| Customer Mobile | Conditional | — | Required if Customer Email is not provided. |
| Customer Name | No | — | Display name on the checkout page. |

<Note>
  At least one of **Customer Email** or **Customer Mobile** is required so PayGlocal can dispatch the link. Providing both enables delivery over both channels.
</Note>
