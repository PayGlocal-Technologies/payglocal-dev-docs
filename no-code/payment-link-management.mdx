## What you can do

From the **Payment Link** tab in GCC:

- **View** every Payment Link you have generated, with its current status.
- **Search** by `PL_ID` (Payment Link ID) or `merchantTxnID`.
- **Filter** by status, date range, or amount.
- **Download** the filtered view as a CSV.
- **Disable** an active link to prevent further payment attempts.
- **View attempts** to see each payment attempt made on a specific link.

## Payment Link status

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| `Active` | Link is live and accepting payments. |
| `Transacted` | A payment has been successfully completed on the link. |
| `Exhausted` | 10 payment attempts have been made; the link is no longer usable. |
| `Expired` | The link's expiry window has passed. |
| `Disabled` | You manually disabled the link from the dashboard. |

<Note>
  A link transitions automatically to `Transacted`, `Exhausted`, or `Expired`. Only `Disabled` is a manual action.
</Note>
