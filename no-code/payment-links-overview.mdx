## What is a Payment Link?

A Payment Link is a unique URL that collects a payment from a specific customer for a specific amount. You share the link over email, SMS, WhatsApp, or any channel, and the customer completes payment on a PayGlocal-hosted checkout page.

## How it works

1. **Generate** a link from the GCC dashboard with amount, currency, customer contact, and expiry.
2. PayGlocal **emails and/or SMSes** the link to the customer automatically (if contact provided).
3. Customer opens the link and pays on the hosted page.
4. You **track status** (Active, Transacted, Exhausted, Expired, Disabled) in the Payment Link dashboard.
