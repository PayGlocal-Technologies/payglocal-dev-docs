## No Code Overview {#no-code-overview}

No-code PayGlocal lets you collect money through **hosted experiences** and dashboard-driven configuration:

| Product | One-line purpose |
|---------|------------------|
| **Payment Link** | Share a URL; customer pays on PayGlocal-hosted checkout. |
| **Payment Invoice Link** | Invoice-style request-for-payment with the same hosted checkout pattern. |
| **Payment Button** | A button or snippet that launches checkout for a configured amount. |

**Prerequisites:** GCC access for the right environment (UAT vs Production). See [Dashboard & key setup](/getting-started/dashboard-and-key-management).

## Payment Link {#payment-link}

### Why use Payment Link

- You need to collect a **fixed amount** without backend integration.
- You want PayGlocal to **host checkout** and handle payment method UX.
- You share the link over **email, SMS, WhatsApp**, or any channel.

### How it works

1. In GCC, create a link with **amount**, **currency**, optional **customer contact**, and **expiry**.
2. PayGlocal can **email/SMS** the link when contacts are provided.
3. The customer completes payment on the **hosted page**.
4. You track **status** in GCC (for example Active, Transacted, Exhausted, Expired, Disabled).

### How to create it

1. Sign in to **GCC** for the correct environment.
2. Open the **Payment Links** (or equivalent) area — exact labels depend on your GCC version; your **account manager** can confirm the menu path.
3. Fill required fields and **generate** the link.
4. Copy or send the link; monitor status on the Payment Link dashboard.

## Payment Invoice Link {#payment-invoice-link}

### Why use Payment Invoice Link

- You are collecting against an **invoice** or formal **request for payment**.
- You want copy and framing suited to **billed amounts** and invoice context while still using **hosted checkout**.

### How it works

- Same operational pattern as Payment Link: configure in GCC, share the link, customer pays on PayGlocal, you reconcile in the dashboard.
- Exact fields and templates will match your GCC release — screenshots below will document the live UI.

### How to create it

1. In GCC, open the **invoice link** (or similarly named) product area.
2. Enter **invoice / billing** details per your process (reference numbers, line narrative if applicable).
3. Publish or send the link through your chosen channel.

## Payment Button {#payment-button}

### Why use Payment Button

- You want a **minimal embed** (button or snippet) on a landing page or storefront **without** a full API integration.
- Checkout should still be **PayGlocal-hosted** after the click.

### How it works

1. Configure the payment in **GCC** and obtain the **button** or embed snippet PayGlocal provides.
2. Place the control on your surface; the customer is sent to **hosted checkout** when they click.
3. Track outcomes in GCC.

### How to create it

1. Sign in to GCC → open the **Payment Button** (or equivalent) product area.
2. Set **amount**, **currency**, and any required metadata.
3. Copy the **snippet** or download the asset your AM provides; deploy on your site within your CSP rules.
