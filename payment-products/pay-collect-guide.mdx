## PayCollect deep dive {#paycollect-deep-dive}

### What is PayCollect? {#what-is-paycollect}

<div className="not-prose payg-prose-callout rounded-xl border border-gray-200 bg-gray-50/80 px-5 py-4 text-sm leading-relaxed text-gray-800 dark:border-white/10 dark:bg-zinc-900/60 dark:text-gray-200">
  PayCollect is a <strong>hosted payment page</strong> solution: PayGlocal manages the collection experience. Customers are redirected to a <strong>secure PayGlocal page</strong> to enter card details, which reduces your security and compliance obligations compared with collecting PAN on your own domain.
</div>
