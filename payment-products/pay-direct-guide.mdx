### What is PayGlocal PayDirect? {#what-is-paydirect}

<div className="not-prose payg-prose-callout rounded-xl border border-gray-200 bg-gray-50/80 px-5 py-4 text-sm leading-relaxed text-gray-800 dark:border-white/10 dark:bg-zinc-900/60 dark:text-gray-200">
  PayGlocal <strong>PayDirect</strong> is a <strong>direct payment integration</strong> for <strong>PCI DSS compliant</strong> merchants who collect card data on <strong>their own interface</strong>.
