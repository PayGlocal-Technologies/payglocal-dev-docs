<Note>Apple Pay on PayGlocal is available through [PayCollect](/payments/paycollect/overview) only. No separate Apple Pay developer account or domain verification is required when using PayCollect.</Note>

## How It Works

When Apple Pay is enabled for your Merchant ID, PayGlocal's hosted checkout automatically detects the customer's device and browser capabilities and surfaces Apple Pay as a payment option. The entire Apple Pay token exchange happens server-side between PayGlocal and Apple — no additional integration work is required on the merchant side.

Your integration remains the same standard GPI flow. The callback you receive is identical regardless of whether the customer paid via Apple Pay or another method.

## Requirements

- PayCollect integration (not available for PayDirect)
- Apple Pay enabled for your Merchant ID (contact your account manager)
- Customer on Safari on an Apple device with Apple Pay configured

## Testing

Apple Pay cannot be fully tested in the PayGlocal sandbox environment. Test Apple Pay in production using a real Apple device with a small transaction amount.

<Note>To enable Apple Pay for your Merchant ID, contact merchant.support@payglocal.in.</Note>
