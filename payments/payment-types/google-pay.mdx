<Note>Google Pay on PayGlocal is available through [PayCollect](/payments/paycollect/overview). When enabled, it appears automatically in the hosted checkout payment method list.</Note>

## How It Works

PayGlocal handles the Google Pay token exchange server-side. When the customer selects Google Pay on the PayGlocal hosted checkout, PayGlocal processes the Google Pay payment token and initiates the charge. Your integration remains the standard GPI flow — the callback you receive is identical to any other payment.

## Requirements

- PayCollect integration
- Google Pay enabled for your Merchant ID (contact your account manager)
- Customer has Google Pay set up on their device

## Testing in Sandbox

Google Pay can be tested in the PayGlocal sandbox using Google Pay test cards. In the UAT hosted checkout, select Google Pay and use a test card from your Google Pay test environment.

<Note>For Google Pay with PayDirect (custom checkout), contact merchant.support@payglocal.in — additional configuration is required.</Note>
