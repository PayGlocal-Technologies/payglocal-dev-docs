## Prerequisites

- An OpenCart store with admin access.
- PayGlocal merchant credentials (available from your [GCC dashboard](/getting-started/gcc-dashboard)).
- The PayGlocal extension archive — contact your PayGlocal account manager to obtain the latest build.

## Install

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the extension">
    Obtain the latest `payglocal-opencart.ocmod.zip` from your PayGlocal account manager.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload">
    In the OpenCart admin, go to **Extensions → Installer** and upload the archive.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refresh modifications">
    Go to **Extensions → Modifications** and click **Refresh**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install payment method">
    Go to **Extensions → Extensions → Payments**, locate **PayGlocal**, and click **Install**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure">
    Click **Edit** on the PayGlocal payment method. Enter your API Key and RSA keys, set status to **Enabled**, and save.
  </Step>
</Steps>
