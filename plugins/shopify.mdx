<Info>
  Shopify merchants install PayGlocal directly from the Shopify App Store — no ZIP download or file upload required.
</Info>

## Prerequisites

- An active Shopify store.
- PayGlocal merchant credentials (available from your [GCC dashboard](/getting-started/gcc-dashboard)).

## Install

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find PayGlocal in the Shopify App Store">
    Search for **PayGlocal** in the Shopify App Store.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the app">
    Click **Add app** and authorize PayGlocal to access your store.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter credentials">
    In the PayGlocal app settings, enter your API Key and upload your RSA keys.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable at checkout">
    Go to **Settings → Payments** in your Shopify admin and enable PayGlocal as a payment provider.
  </Step>
</Steps>
