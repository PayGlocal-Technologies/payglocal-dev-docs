## Prerequisites

- A WordPress site with WooCommerce installed.
- PayGlocal merchant credentials (available from your [GCC dashboard](/getting-started/gcc-dashboard)).
- The PayGlocal plugin ZIP — contact your PayGlocal account manager to obtain the latest build.

## Install

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the plugin ZIP">
    Obtain the latest `payglocal-woocommerce.zip` from your PayGlocal account manager.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload to WordPress">
    In the WordPress admin, go to **Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin** and select the ZIP.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate">
    Click **Activate** once the upload completes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure">
    Go to **WooCommerce → Settings → Payments**, select **PayGlocal**, and enter your API Key and RSA keys.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable">
    Toggle PayGlocal to **Enabled** and save.
  </Step>
</Steps>
