---
title: "Business Categories"
description: "Complete reference of all business category codes and subcategory codes for the natureOfBusiness field."
---

Use the **subcategory code** (dotted format, e.g., `BUS.IT_SERVICES`) as the value for the `natureOfBusiness` field in [Update Business Details](/api-reference/update-business-details).

You can also fetch this list dynamically via the [Get Business Categories API](/api-reference/get-business-categories).

---
