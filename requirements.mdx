---
title: "Requirements from Partner"
description: "Compliance obligations every partner must fulfill before using the PayGlocal Merchant Onboarding API."
---

As a regulated payment aggregator, PayGlocal operates under master directions issued by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI). To remain compliant, **all partners must meet the following six requirements** before — and during — their integration.

<Warning>
  Failure to meet these requirements may result in suspension of API access. These obligations are non-negotiable and subject to regular audit by PayGlocal's compliance team.
</Warning>

---

## 1. Merchant Disclosure

Partners must display a clear and prominent disclosure to every merchant informing them that their onboarding is being processed on the **PayGlocal platform**.

This disclosure must be placed at an appropriate point in the partner's onboarding flow — before the merchant submits any personal or business information. The wording and placement must be approved as part of the compliance review (see Requirement 2).

<Warning>
  Embedding the onboarding flow without this disclosure is a compliance violation. PayGlocal may reject or suspend the integration if this requirement is not met.
</Warning>

---

## 2. Compliance Review of the Flow

The complete merchant onboarding user flow — including all screens, disclosures, and data collection steps — must be **submitted to PayGlocal for compliance review** before the integration goes live.

Submit your flow screens or prototype to your assigned PayGlocal Account Manager. PayGlocal will review for adherence to regulatory requirements and provide written approval.

---

## 3. Partner Due Diligence Form

A **partner due diligence form** must be submitted to PayGlocal as part of the partner onboarding process. This form covers your organization's business details, compliance posture, and technical capabilities.

Contact [merchant.support@payglocal.in](mailto:merchant.support@payglocal.in) to receive the due diligence form.

---

## 4. End-to-End Flow Acceptance

Once the integration is technically complete, partners must undergo a **full end-to-end acceptance test** with PayGlocal before going live in Production.

This involves:
- Demonstrating the complete onboarding flow in the Sandbox environment
- Confirming all API calls function correctly
- Receiving written go-live approval from PayGlocal

Do not point to the Production environment until this acceptance is signed off.

---

## 5. Unique Merchant Identity in Partner System

Every entity that is onboarded via the PayGlocal API must have a **unique identity within the partner's own system**. The `externalOnboardingId` sent in the Create Onboarding request must be a stable, unique identifier for that merchant in your platform.

<Warning>
  Do not reuse `externalOnboardingId` values across different merchants or onboarding attempts. Duplicate IDs will result in errors and may flag the integration for audit.
</Warning>

---

## 6. No Validation-Only Usage

Partners **must not use the PayGlocal API for data validation purposes** without a genuine intent to onboard the merchant. The API is designed for active onboarding flows only.

PayGlocal monitors infrastructure-level metrics comparing onboarding initiations against completions. A persistently high initiation-to-completion gap will trigger an audit.

<Warning>
  Using the API solely to validate PAN numbers, business details, or other merchant data — without completing the onboarding — is a breach of the partner agreement and subject to access revocation.
</Warning>

---

## Summary Checklist

| # | Requirement | Who Completes It |
|---|-------------|-----------------|
| 1 | Merchant disclosure shown in partner UI | Partner |
| 2 | Onboarding flow submitted for compliance review | Partner → PayGlocal |
| 3 | Partner due diligence form submitted | Partner → PayGlocal |
| 4 | End-to-end acceptance test passed | Partner + PayGlocal |
| 5 | Unique `externalOnboardingId` per merchant | Partner |
| 6 | API used for genuine onboarding only | Partner |

---

For questions about any of these requirements, contact [merchant.support@payglocal.in](mailto:merchant.support@payglocal.in).
