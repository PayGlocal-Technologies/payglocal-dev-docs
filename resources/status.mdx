## Checking Service Status

For real-time API availability and incident updates, contact merchant.support@payglocal.in or check with your account manager for the current status page URL.

## During an Incident

If you suspect a PayGlocal service issue:

1. Check your recent API error rates and response codes
2. Verify your credentials and request signing are correct (rule out client-side issues first)
3. Check webhook delivery logs for delays
4. Contact merchant.support@payglocal.in with your Merchant ID and observed error pattern

## Production Incident Escalation

For issues actively affecting live payment processing:

**Email:** merchant.support@payglocal.in  
**Subject line:** `PRODUCTION INCIDENT - <your MID> - <brief description>`

Include:
- Your Merchant ID
- Affected endpoint(s)
- Error codes and response bodies
- Approximate start time
- Transaction volume affected

## Scheduled Maintenance

PayGlocal notifies registered contacts at least 48 hours before scheduled maintenance windows. Contact your account manager to ensure your technical contact is registered for maintenance notifications.

## Webhook Delivery During Incidents

If PayGlocal is experiencing delivery delays, webhooks will be retried after the incident resolves. Always implement the [Get Transaction Status](/api-reference/payments-v2/common/get-transaction-status) fallback rather than relying solely on webhook delivery.
