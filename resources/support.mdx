## Technical Integration Support

For API errors, integration questions, and debugging:

**Email:** merchant.support@payglocal.in

Include in your support request:
- Merchant ID
- The affected GID, onboardingId, or mandateId
- Error code and full response body
- Endpoint called (method + path)
- Timestamp of the issue
- Steps to reproduce (if possible)

**Response SLA:** 1 business day for standard issues, same-day for production-impacting issues.

## Production Incident Escalation

For issues actively affecting live payment processing:

**Email:** merchant.support@payglocal.in  
**Subject:** `PRODUCTION INCIDENT - <your MID> - <brief description>`

## Business and Account Queries

For commercial questions, MID activation, feature enablement (Apple Pay, DCC, MCA), and account setup:

Contact your named **PayGlocal account manager** directly.

If you do not have a named account manager, email merchant.support@payglocal.in with subject "Account Manager Assignment Request."
