<Warning>
  `riskData` is **required for international card transactions** and strongly recommended for all
  transactions. It is processed by PayGlocal's risk engine to help prevent fraudulent transactions
  and improve approval rates.
</Warning>

**RuPay cards** additionally require `ipAddress`, `httpAccept`, and `httpUserAgent` inside
`riskData.customerData`.

The `riskData` block accepts four sub-objects:

| Sub-object | When required | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| `customerData` | Preferred for all | Customer account info, IP address, browser headers |
| `shippingData` | Required for e-commerce (physical goods) | Delivery address and contact details |
| `orderData[]` | Preferred for all | Line items: product description, SKU, type, unit price, quantity |
| `flightData[]` | Required for airline / travel | Flight booking, leg, and passenger data |

See [Risk Data Fields](/api-fields/risk-data-fields) for the complete field reference.
